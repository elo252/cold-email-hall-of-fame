The Cold Email Hall of Fame

Cold emails, calls and letters that got answers from people who weren't supposed to answer: Bill Gates, Mark Cuban, Marc Benioff, Elon Musk, Sundar Pichai and more. Each one is summarized, tagged by the tactic that made it work, and linked to its source.

What the winners have in common

How often each tactic shows up across the archive. Click one to see every email that used it.

  1. One ask, small and specific. “Three tips,” “can I send more,” “interview for my paper.” Never “pick your brain.”
  2. Credibility in the first line. A backer, a metric, a byline, a hard thing you did.
  3. Short enough for a phone. Most winners are under 150 words. Hagerman's fit in the subject line.
  4. Research that proves you're not spamming. Their public statements, their old story, their whiskey.
  5. Arrive when they're primed. New fund, new company, a blog post asking for exactly you.
  6. Give before you take. A demo, an essay, free strategy, a video shot on spec.
  7. Follow up with something new. Stebbings needed 53. Each one changed the hook.
